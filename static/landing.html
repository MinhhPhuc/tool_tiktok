How it works

  1. Connect. Sign in with TikTok and approve access for each account. You can connect as many accounts as you are authorized to manage.
  2. Prepare. Choose a video, write a caption, select the accounts, and set who can view the post. You can also schedule it.
  3. Publish. The video is uploaded through the official TikTok Content Posting API and posted, or saved to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft for you to finish in the TikTok app.

What we access

  • Your TikTok display name and avatar, only to label each connected account in the dashboard.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos that you choose, only when you ask us to.

We never post on your behalf without an action from you, and you can disconnect an account at any time from the dashboard or in your TikTok app permissions. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
