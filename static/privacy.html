This policy explains what information [APP NAME] (the "Service") collects when you connect TikTok accounts and publish videos, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information. When you connect an account through TikTok Login, we receive your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues to us, together with the permissions (scopes) you approved.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to train machine learning models.

We send your videos, captions and tokens to TikTok only to carry out the actions you request. We do not share your information with other third parties, except hosting or infrastructure providers that process it on our behalf, or when the law requires it.

4. Storage, retention and security

Access tokens and post records are stored on our server with restricted access. Uploaded videos are kept only until the related posts are completed or removed from the queue, and are then deleted. When you disconnect an account, we delete its tokens and profile information. We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no system is completely secure.
